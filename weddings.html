I’m Kathy, a wedding officiant in Toronto. I write and lead ceremonies that are personal, warm and unhurried — built around your story rather than a template.

My approach

Personal, and genuinely inclusive

All couples are welcome here — every love, every background, every kind of family. I take the time to understand who you are together, then shape a ceremony around it: the words, the traditions you want to keep or leave behind, and the moments that matter most to you.

Nothing about your day should feel borrowed. My job is to hold the space so it feels like yours.

What working together looks like

Three simple steps

  1. 01

    A conversation

    We meet — in person or by video — to talk about the two of you and the kind of ceremony you’re hoping for. No pressure, just a chance to see if we’re a fit.

  2. 02

    Crafting the ceremony

    I write a draft and we shape it together — the story, the vows, the readings and the rituals — until it reads and feels exactly right.

  3. 03

    The day

    I arrive early, take care of the details, and lead a calm, present ceremony so you can simply be in the moment with the people you love.
