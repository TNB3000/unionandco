About

I build experiences around people, not scripts.

I’m Kathy Marcelline. Whether I’m guiding visitors through Toronto or standing with a couple on their wedding day, the work is the same at heart: pay attention to the people in front of me, and make something that fits them.

Toronto is a city best understood through the people who live in it and the people who come to see it. Over the years I’ve spent guiding groups, welcoming visitors and hosting programs, I’ve learned that a good day is rarely about covering the most ground. It’s about reading the group, finding the thread that matters to them, and following it.

That same instinct is what drew me to officiating. A wedding isn’t a checklist either — it’s two people and a story. Tourism and ceremonies might look like different lines of work, but they come from the same place for me: bringing people together, and being genuinely present while I do it.

What guides the work

Three things I hold to

Understand the people first

Before the itinerary or the wording, I listen. Who is this really for, and what do they need the day to feel like?

Stay true to the story

Whether it’s a city or a couple, the good version is the honest one. I’d rather be authentic than impressive.

Bring people together

The best part of this work is connection — between visitors and a place, between partners, between people who might not otherwise meet.
